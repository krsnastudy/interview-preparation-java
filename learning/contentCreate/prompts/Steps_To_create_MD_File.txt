Use InterviewHandbook.md as a starting point for one topic at a time:
1. Make a copy of the file.
2. Replace [Topic Name] and other bracketed prompts with your topic and details.
3. Replace the sample code and diagrams with examples that fit.
4. Remove sections that genuinely don’t apply, and replace the example resource links with useful links for that topic.
For SRE, a focused topic works better than trying to cover all of Site Reliability Engineering at once. 
For example, make the handbook about SLOs, SLIs, and error budgets.
